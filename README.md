# The Definition of Space in the Landscape

> An interactive parametric design studio for exploring how geometry, planting, walls, and sections shape landscape space.

![The Definition of Space in the Landscape cover](assets/images/the-definition-of-space-in-the-landscape-cover.jpg)

## Overview

**The Definition of Space in the Landscape** is a teaching-oriented design tool for introductory landscape architecture and urban design studios. It transforms abstract spatial-composition exercises into an interactive workflow: students construct a geometric ground pattern, introduce arcs, arrange planting, define walls and openings, then examine their proposal in plan, section, and axonometric views.

The project supports the course theme **“The Definition of Space in the Landscape”**—using clear rules and editable parameters to help students test spatial order, movement, enclosure, and scale.

## Design workflow

| Stage | Focus | Learning outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Grid composition** | Create horizontal and vertical divisions with controlled proportions and a maximum three-colour composition. |
| 02 | **Arc geometry** | Add arcs from legible geometric anchors and combine them with straight divisions to generate spatial rhythm. |
| 03 | **Planting arrays** | Test tree grids, rows, and solitary planting; adjust quantity, spacing, offset, and alignment. |
| 04 | **Walls & sections** | Introduce low walls and openings, then evaluate enclosure and spatial sequence through sections and axonometric drawings. |

![The Definition of Space in the Landscape feature overview](assets/images/the-definition-of-space-in-the-landscape-feature-overview.jpg)

## Core features

- Parametric horizontal, vertical, and curved ground divisions.
- Editable planting arrays, tree rows, and individual trees with spacing annotations.
- Intuitive wall placement along divisions, site boundaries, and curves; adjustable openings.
- Multiple editable section lines with plan, section/elevation, and axonometric views.
- Zoomable drawing views with scale bars and PNG exports.
- Layer controls for visibility, locking, ordering, duplication, and deletion.

## Spatial rules

The tool is deliberately constrained so that design decisions remain visible:

- Use no more than three ground-pattern colours.
- Keep wall heights at or below **2.4 m**.
- Keep the total length of movable walls within **one quarter** of the site perimeter/length constraint.
- Keep wall openings within **12 m²** in total, using fewer openings when possible.

## Educational context

Designed for landscape and urban design education, the studio connects planar composition with built spatial experience. It is suited to exercises on:

- spatial definition and visual order;
- planting as a structural design element;
- sequence, permeability, and enclosure;
- reading a design consistently across plan, section, and axonometric representation.
